# Privacy Policy — Flashcard: Remember Everything

Effective date: October 11, 2026

Pathvana LLC ("we", "us") builds **Flashcard: Remember Everything** ("the app"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

## The short version

The app collects **no personal data**. Your flashcards, decks, and study history are stored on your device only. We have no servers and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device

- Decks and cards you create, review history, and app preferences are saved in the app's local storage on your device.
- Photos you take or choose in the app are processed on your device to recognize text. Nothing is uploaded by us.

## Optional features that use third parties

- **AI card generation:** If you enter your own OpenAI API key in Settings, text you submit for card generation is sent directly from your device to OpenAI under your own OpenAI account. That data is governed by [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy). We never see your key or your text.
- **Apple iCloud:** If you enable iCloud sync for the app through your Apple ID settings, Apple may sync your data between your devices under your iCloud plan and [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Permissions

- **Camera / photo library:** Used only to photograph study material you choose. Images never leave your device except through the optional AI feature above.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page.
